Namoz — намаз и умра includes My Umrah, a step-by-step reference guide with six stages, saved progress and manual tawaf and sa'i counters with undo. Choose your starting situation, read the miqat directory and Arabic cards with source links, and continue in Uzbek Latin, Uzbek Cyrillic, Russian or English.

The guide is bundled for offline reading. It is an introductory reference aid; translations are drafts and independent specialist approval is not claimed. It does not replace a knowledgeable guide. Marking a stage does not establish the validity of a rite.

An optional local preparation reminder uses your own estimate of time until the miqat and your chosen lead time. Android requires notification and exact-alarm access. It does not detect miqat boundaries or count movements automatically. Optional location is a one-time snapshot with time and accuracy, not route guidance. Progress and My Umrah reminder data stay on the device and are excluded from usage analytics.

Namoz also offers nearby mosque search, on-device prayer calculations, Qibla, daily adhkar, local prayer/event reminders, light/dark themes and home-screen widgets. Nearby results and directions need a network connection and coverage varies. External source pages require internet.

This separate F-Droid edition uses Android LocationManager and contains no Firebase Messaging or Google Play Services SDK. There is no cloud push, advertising or in-app purchase. Optional accounts and some network features use the Namoz server; usage statistics are off by default. See the source repository's privacy information for providers and limitations.
